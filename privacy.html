Monty Minutes Uploader — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026

Monty Minutes Uploader ("the App") is a private desktop tool that publishes the owner's own videos to the owner's own

TikTok account.

What we access: when the owner signs in with TikTok, the App receives an access token and basic profile information

(display name and avatar, scope user.info.basic) and permission to upload and publish videos (scopes video.upload and

video.publish).

How we use it: only to upload and publish videos chosen by the owner, and to show which account is connected.

Storage: the access token is stored only on the owner's own computer. No data is sold, shared with third parties, or

used for advertising. No data about other TikTok users is collected.

Deleting data: the owner can disconnect the App at any time in TikTok (Settings > Security > Apps and services) and

delete the local token file; this removes all data held by the App.

Contact: through the Monty Minutes YouTube channel (youtube.com/@montyminutes).
